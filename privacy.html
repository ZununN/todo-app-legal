Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 November 2026

ToDo: Habit Tracker & Streaks ("the app") is developed by Nurakhmet Zunun.

The app does not collect, store on servers, share or sell any personal data. It has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it does not connect to the internet.

Your tasks, checklists, history and settings are stored only on your device. If you create a backup file, it is saved where you choose, and only you control it. Deleting the app removes its data from the device.

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them.

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page.
